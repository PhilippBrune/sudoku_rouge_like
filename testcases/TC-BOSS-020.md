# TC-BOSS-020: Gameplay Triggers

- **Requirement:** REQ-BOSS-021
- **Type:** Integration
- **Risk:** Medium
- **Created:** 2026-03-22 12:38:56

## Precondition

User has encountered a boss encounter requiring triggers for gameplay events to occur.

## Steps

1. Start a new game with the appropriate conditions (e.g., all relevant areas have been cleared and all necessary modifiers are available).
2. Trigger various gameplay events that should be occurring in response to boss mechanics, such as boss puzzle start/end, modifier intensity changes, fog of war activation and deactivation etc.
3. Verify the system triggers these events correctly at the right times without interfering with other gameplay events.

## Expected Result

The correct gameplay events are triggered at the expected time without conflicting with other gameplay events.
