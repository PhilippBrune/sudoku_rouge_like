# TC-BOSS-033: Gameplay Triggering for Boss Mechanics Testing

- **Requirement:** REQ-BOSS-033
- **Type:** Unit
- **Risk:** Medium
- **Created:** 2026-03-22 12:39:41

## Precondition

Start a new boss run and verify if gameplay events related to boss mechanics trigger correctly.

## Steps

1. Initiate a new boss run.
2. Verify that gameplay events are triggered for boss mechanics at the right times.
3. Interact with the game by trying different inputs and observe how these interfere with other gameplay events.

## Expected Result

Gameplay events for boss mechanics trigger correctly at the right times without interfering with other gameplay events.
