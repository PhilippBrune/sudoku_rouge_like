- **Requirement:** REQ<｜begin▁of▁sentence｜>-003
- **Type:** Manual
- **Risk:** Medium
- **Created:** 2026-03-22 08:52:08

## Precondition

Accessibility Settings are configured to Colorblind Mode

## Steps

1. Navigate back to the Main Menu in the game.
2. Repeat steps 1-3 from previous test case. This time, disable "Colorblind Mode".
3. Play a game or interact with an interface that makes use of color indicators.

## Expected Result

The text and icons used for color cues are no longer visible to users who have Colorblind Mode enabled.
