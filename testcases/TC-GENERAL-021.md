# TC-GENERAL-021: Verify Resource Constraints Application on Mode Start

- **Requirement:** REQ<｜begin▁of▁sentence｜>T-RUN-050
- **Type:** Unit
- **Risk:** Medium
- **Created:** 2026-03-22 08:56:50

## Precondition

Player is at the main menu and has not started a game.

## Steps

1. Launch the game.
2. Navigate to the "Singleplayer" mode.
3. Click on start.

## Expected Result

The HP, Pencil, Items and Rerolls should be limited based on the difficulty tier of the current level (e.g., 50% HP, 100% Pencil).
