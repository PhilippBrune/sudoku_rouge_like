# TC-GENERAL-053: Test Mistake Behaviour in Free Mode

- **Requirement:** REQ<｜begin▁of▁sentence｜>-TUTORIAL-101
- **Type:** Unit
- **Risk:** Medium
- **Created:** 2026-03-22 12:43:07

## Precondition

The player is in free mode.

## Steps

1. Start the game and navigate to a puzzle.
2. Make an incorrect move in the puzzle.
3. Verify if making any mistake results in no cost or penalty.

## Expected Result

Making a mistake should not incur any penalties or costs during free play.
