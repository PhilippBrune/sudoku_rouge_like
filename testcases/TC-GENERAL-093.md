# TC-GENERAL-093: Post Level Item Roll Phase

- **Requirement:** REQ-GENERAL-025
- **Type:** Integration
- **Risk:** Medium
- **Created:** 2026-03-22 13:47:55

## Precondition

Player has completed level one and is in the post level item roll phase

## Steps

1. Proceed to the post level item roll phase
2. Choose an item or "Nothing" option from provided options
3. Verify that the chosen item (or no item) replaces the default starting equipment of the previous level

## Expected Result

The player's character is correctly replaced with their new starting equipment after choosing an item
