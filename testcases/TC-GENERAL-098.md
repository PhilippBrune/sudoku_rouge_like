- **Type:** Integration
- **Risk:** Medium

## Precondition

The game is running and the user has an item in their inventory or equipment slot.

## Steps

1. Launch the game.
2. Select a difficulty level from the main menu.
3. Navigate to an area where items are being dropped (like combat encounters).
4. Drop an item onto the Nothing Slot icon on the top left of the screen.
5. Verify that the item disappears and no error messages appear.

## Expected Result

The game should handle dropping items into the "Nothing" slot properly, removing them from the inventory/equipment slots without causing any errors.
