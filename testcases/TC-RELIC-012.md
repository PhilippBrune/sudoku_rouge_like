# TC-RELIC-012: Test LanternOfClarityItem Fog of War modifier disabling

- **Requirement:** REQ-RELIC-012
- **Type:** Unit
- **Risk:** Medium
- **Created:** 2026-03-22 08:54:47

## Precondition

The game world has active Fog of War modifier.

## Steps

1. Equip the lantern of clarity item to a suitable slot.
2. Verify the fog around the player character is temporarily disabled and other effects based on its rarity are applied.

## Expected Result

After step 2, the fog should be temporarily disabled and an appropriate status effect or feedback should be given based on the rarity of the item.
