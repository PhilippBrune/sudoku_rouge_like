- **Requirement:** REQ-RELIC-013
- **Type:** Unit
- **Risk:** High

The system is running and a unique item has been obtained

1. Pick up the unique item.

3. Verify if the intended effect of the item was applied correctly.

The correct effects are applied to the user's character/items.
