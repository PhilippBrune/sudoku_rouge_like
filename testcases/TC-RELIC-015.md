# TC-RELIC-015: Test ItemSelectionFromRewardsScreen

- **Requirement:** REQ-RELIC-016
- **Type:** Manual
- **Risk:** Low
- **Created:** 2026-03-22 13:49:05

## Precondition

The game is running and a rewards screen is available at different star ratings based on difficulty.

## Steps

1. Launch the game.
2. Select a difficulty level from the main menu.
3. Navigate to the rewards screen.
4. Look at the displayed items of varying star ratings for each category (item, relic).
5. Pick up an item with the highest star rating in an uncommon category that is not already on your equipment/inventory slot.

## Expected Result

The game should provide a mechanism to select items based on difficulty and their rarity level from the rewards screen. Items of higher star ratings should be more likely to appear when choosing a high difficulty.
