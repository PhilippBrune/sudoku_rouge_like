# TC-RUN-013: Steam Leaderboard Setup and Score Submission

- **Requirement:** REQ-RUN-013
- **Type:** Integration
- **Risk:** High
- **Created:** 2026-03-22 08:53:42

## Precondition

Player has completed the game at least once, and they are logged into their steam account.

## Steps

1. Launch the "Run of the Nine" Unity game.
2. Complete a certain level in Endless Zen mode.
3. Submit score on Steam Leaderboard via in-game menu.

## Expected Result

The game should successfully submit the player's score to their connected steam account leaderboard and confirm the submission.
