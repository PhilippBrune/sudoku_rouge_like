# TC-RUN-015: High Score Comparison and Display

- **Requirement:** REQ-RUN-016
- **Type:** Integration
- **Risk:** Low
- **Created:** 2026-03-22 08:53:42

## Precondition

Player has achieved a new high score after completing a level.

## Steps

1. Complete a level in Endless Zen mode.
2. Submit the obtained score to Steam Leaderboard via in-game menu.
3. Verify that the displayed leaderboard shows updated top scores including your own.

## Expected Result

The game should correctly update and display the new high score on the main leaderboard after a successful submission.
