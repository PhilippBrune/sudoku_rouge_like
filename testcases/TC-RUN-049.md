# TC-RUN-049: Verify Steam Workshop Leaderboards Integration

- **Requirement:** REQ-RUN-049
- **Type:** Integration
- **Risk:** High
- **Created:** 2026-03-22 08:56:50

## Precondition

Player is logged into their steam account and has an active workshop collection.

## Steps

1. Launch the game.
2. Login to a steam account with a valid workshop collection.
3. Open the main menu, navigate to the leaderboards section.

## Expected Result

The top 10 players from across all collections should be displayed in the leaderboard.
