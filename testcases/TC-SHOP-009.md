# TC-SHOP-009: Testing the Nothing Slot Mechanism

- **Requirement:** REQ-SHOP-009
- **Type:** Unit
- **Risk:** Low
- **Created:** 2026-03-22 08:54:09

## Precondition

Game is running with an item/relic to be bought from nothing slot.

## Steps

1. Open the shop menu.
2. Attempt to buy a single item or relic using nothing as payment.
3. Verify that no new items are added and selecting the nothing slot provides no reward or bonus.

## Expected Result

Buying an item/relic from the nothing slot should result in ending the item reward phase without any additional rewards being granted.
