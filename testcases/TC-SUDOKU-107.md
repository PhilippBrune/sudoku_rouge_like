# TC-SUDOKU-107: ModifierGeometryGenerationComparisonTest

- **Requirement:** REQ-SUDOKU-108
- **Type:** Unit
- **Risk:** High
- **Created:** 2026-03-22 12:42:09

## Precondition

Set up a scenario with varying numbers of active bosses encountered.

## Steps

1. Monitor the generation of puzzles in different instances, each time having different levels of active bosses.
2. Observe how modifier geometry is generated based on number of active bosses and puzzle difficulty.
3. Analyze the results to determine if modifier geometry generation is consistently affected by varying numbers of active bosses.

## Expected Result

The variation in the geometry of modifiers should be influenced by varying levels of active bosses encountered, affecting puzzle difficulty and variability.
