# TC-SUDOKU-116: Impact of Irregular Template Expansion on Puzzle Variety

- **Requirement:** REQ-SUDOKU-116
- **Type:** Integration
- **Risk:** Low
- **Created:** 2026-03-22 12:42:31

## Precondition

User has initiated a new puzzle game with template expansion enabled

## Steps

1. Start a new game with template expansion enabled
2. Compare the resulting puzzles to those without template expansion
3. Progress through the game, ensuring puzzles are being correctly generated and unique

## Expected Result

Puzzle generation process should produce more varied puzzles when irregular templates are used
