# TC-TUTORIAL-121: VerifyFreeResourceModeDoesNotApplyClassStatsRestrictions

- **Requirement:** REQ-TUTORIAL-120
- **Type:** Unit
- **Risk:** Medium
- **Created:** 2026-03-22 12:43:52

## Precondition

Player is in tutorial mode and class has non zero stats.

## Steps

1. Start a new game in Free Resource Mode.
2. Solve the first puzzle.
3. Verify that the player's stats have not been altered by the restrictions applied to them from their selected class during the tutorial.

## Expected Result

Player's stats should remain unaffected, as they are not impacted by these restrictions in Free Resource Mode.
