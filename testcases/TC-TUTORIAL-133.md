# TC-TUTORIAL-133: MistakeBehaviourFreeMode

- **Requirement:** REQ-TUTORIAL-132
- **Type:** Unit
- **Risk:** Medium
- **Created:** 2026-03-22 12:44:18

## Precondition

User has entered Free resource mode.

## Steps

1. Play the game in Free resource mode.
2. Make a mistake, check if any resources are deducted or penalties applied to the user.
3. Verify that mistakes do not have an adverse impact on their progression.

## Expected Result

Mistakes should be free of cost and incur no penalty during Free resource mode.
