# TC-TUTORIAL-162: TestProgressPersistenceInFreeResourceMode

- **Requirement:** REQ-TUTORIAL-162
- **Type:** Integration
- **Risk:** High
- **Created:** 2026-03-22 12:45:32

## Precondition

Player has started a game in Free Resource Mode and completed some puzzles.

## Steps

1. Close the application or browser tab while playing.
2. Reopen the application/game and continue from where they left off.
3. Verify that their progress is not lost and puzzles are unfinished.

## Expected Result

Progress of completing different configurations should persist across sessions without any loss.
