# TC-UI-030: Test SoundDesignPhilosophy

- **Requirement:** REQ-UI-031
- **Type:** Integration
- **Risk:** High
- **Created:** 2026-03-22 12:37:53

## Precondition

The game is open and running on a particular floor with sound enabled

## Steps

1. Open the game and navigate to a specific floor
2. Play the game with sound effects turned on
3. Listen to sounds, check if they match the predefined natural materials tonal palette (wood, wind, water, stone)

## Expected Result

Sounds follow a natural materials tonal palette in sound design and feel like they belong in a physical garden space
