# FAQ

**Run of the Nine**

**What platforms is it on?**
Windows, on Steam: https://store.steampowered.com/app/4858470

**Which languages does it support?**
English, German, French, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. French, Spanish and Portuguese were translated recently; if a line reads wrong, post it in #rotn-bugs with the tag "Translation".

**Can I play with a controller?**
Yes. Menus, the board and the Options work with a controller; the on-screen number pad enters digits.

**Where are my saves?**
`%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Kratossos\sudoku_rouge_like` (three profile slots). Steam Cloud keeps them in sync.

**A puzzle looks unsolvable. What now?**
Every puzzle has exactly one solution. Post a screenshot in #rotn-bugs with your game version and, if you have it, the run seed (shown in the in-game Options). That helps us check it.

**How do I report a bug?**
One post per bug in #rotn-bugs: what happened, what you expected, the steps, the game version, and a screenshot if you can.

**Corebound**

**What is Corebound?**
An action roguelite: you protect a living energy Core through five stages. You fight directly with up to six weapons at once, build and upgrade four tower types between waves, draft cards that change your weapons, towers and the Core, and turn your defence into production with a Factory chain that pays Scrap.
Steam: https://store.steampowered.com/app/5420920

**When can I play it?**
Corebound comes to Steam in Early Access; the planned date is shown on the store page. Wishlisting helps a lot.

**What is in Early Access?**
Five classes, five stages and five bosses, 42 cards plus ten class cards, a skill tree per class, and Paragon and Ascension for the long run. The game grows from there, and your feedback decides a lot of what comes next.

**What platforms and languages?**
Windows on Steam. English and German (text; there is no voice acting).

**Can I play with a controller?**
Yes. Corebound is built controller-first, with full mouse and keyboard support. Steam Deck is not verified yet.

**Are my saves backed up?**
Yes, Steam Cloud keeps them in sync.

**Does it have achievements?**
Yes, 40 Steam achievements.

**How do I report a bug?**
One post per bug in #cb-bugs: what happened, what you expected, the steps, the game version (shown in the main menu), and a screenshot or clip if you can.

**How can I help?**
Want to playtest or translate? Pick it under "Want to help?" in **Channels & Roles** (top of the channel list); a Moderator then gives you the Playtester or Translator role.

**Can I show where I am from?**
Yes: pick your country in **Channels & Roles** and its flag appears on your profile. Missing yours? Ask a Moderator.
