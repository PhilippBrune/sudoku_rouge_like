# How to play

**Run of the Nine** <:run_of_the_nine:>

A Sudoku roguelike through a Japanese garden. Every row, column and region holds each digit once — the regions are not always boxes, so follow the bold borders.

**A run**
• Five floors. On each floor you walk the garden path and pick the next tile: the calm route or the risk route (bigger rewards, more danger).
• Tiles are puzzles, elites, shops, rest stops, relics, events and cursed tiles. At the end of a floor waits its boss.
• A wrong digit costs HP; pencil marks are limited. Lose all HP and the run ends — your class still keeps the XP you earned.
• Before a boss you pick its trials: choose N of N+1 modifiers (Killer Cages, Thermo lines, Whispers and many more). From floor 3 the boss also brings a debuff.
• Items go in your bag and are used once; relics stay for the whole run.

**Classes and modes**
• Eight classes with their own HP, pencil, bag and passive. They level up and unlock new things.
• Harmony makes runs harder in steps. Daily Walk gives three goals a day, the Monthly Walk one hard 9×9 board for everyone, plus Endless Zen, Spirit Trials, custom puzzles and the Sudoku Basics tutorial.
• Mouse, keyboard or controller.

**Corebound** <:corebound:>

Protect a living energy Core through five stages. Fight with up to six weapons at once, build and upgrade towers between waves, draft cards that change your weapons, towers and the Core, and turn your defence into production with a Factory chain that pays Scrap. Five classes, five bosses, a skill tree per class. Built controller-first, full mouse and keyboard support.
