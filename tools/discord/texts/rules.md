# Server rules

1. **Be kind.** No harassment, insults, hate speech or discrimination. Disagree with ideas, not people.
2. **Keep it safe for everyone.** No sexual, violent or shocking content, in messages, names or avatars.
3. **No spam.** No repeated messages, mass mentions, or invite links to other servers. Self-promotion only when someone asks for it.
4. **No piracy or key trading.** No cracked builds, no selling or begging for keys.
5. **Right channel, one topic per post.** Bug reports and ideas go into the game's bug and feedback channels, so nothing gets lost.
6. **Spoilers in spoiler tags.** Write ||like this|| for late floors, bosses and secrets.
7. **English in public channels**, so everyone can follow. Translation threads may use their own language.
8. **Never share personal data**, yours or anyone else's, and never post account passwords, keys or beta passwords.
9. **Follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines** (https://discord.com/terms, https://discord.com/guidelines).
10. **Moderators have the final say.** If something feels wrong, message a Moderator instead of arguing in public.

Breaking the rules can mean a warning, a timeout or a ban, depending on how serious it is.
