# Welcome to Kratossos

This is the home of two games by Kratossos:

**Run of the Nine**: a Sudoku roguelike. Each solved board takes you one step deeper into a Japanese garden: five floors, branching paths, bosses that bend the rules, and only so much HP and pencil to get there.
Steam: https://store.steampowered.com/app/4858470

**Corebound**: fight, build and engineer your way through five stages to protect a living energy Core. Twin-stick combat with deckbuilding, tower defence and a production network.
